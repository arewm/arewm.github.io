I'm Andrew McNamara, a software supply chain architect passionate about usable CI/CD, 
security, and DevSecOps. I draw from my experience of building and shipping 
containerized software at IBM and Red Hat.

All opinions expressed here are my own.

## What I Do

As a SLSA maintainer, I help people identify how to approach and understand supply 
chain security. I work at Red Hat as a contributor to the Konflux CI project, 
providing developer flexibility without sacrificing software security.

My work focuses on:
- Supply chain security and SLSA (Supply-chain Levels for Software Artifacts)
- CI/CD systems and developer tooling
- DevSecOps practices and implementation
- Containerized software development and deployment

## Experience

I have extensive experience building and shipping containerized software at major 
organizations including IBM and Red Hat. My focus is on creating secure, usable 
systems that empower developers while maintaining high security standards.

## Interests & Community Involvement

- Konflux CI project at Red Hat
- Technical presentations and knowledge sharing
- Speaking at conferences and community events
